---
title: "Homemade Fries"
description: "Crispy oven-baked russet potato fries."
category: "Sides"
cookTime: "PT30M"
servings: "2 servings"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "2/3 russet potatoes"
instructions:
  - "Wash and slice potatoes to desired thickness."
  - "Toss sliced fries in oil, and season to taste."
  - "Arrange on a nonstick baking sheet so none are overlapped, using two trays as needed to give the fries adequate space."
  - "Cook at 450 F."
  - "Flip fries after 15-20 minutes for an even bake."
  - "Cook the remaining time up to 30 minutes total or until desired crispness is reached."
