---
title: "Southwest Chicken and Bacon Wraps"
description: "Grilled marinated chicken wraps loaded with crispy bacon, cheese, and Southwest ranch."
category: "Dinner"
cookTime: "PT2H10M"
servings: "8 wraps"
credit: "https://thrivinghomeblog.com/healthy-southwest-chicken-and-bacon-wrap/"
ingredients:
  - "2 pounds chicken tenders or chicken breasts"
  - "1 batch Southwest Chicken Marinade"
  - "8 slices (about 1/2 lb) bacon, cooked and crumbled"
  - "1 cup shredded Mexican cheese blend or cheddar"
  - "Shredded lettuce"
  - "Diced tomatoes"
  - "1 batch Creamy Southwest Ranch Sauce (or 1:1 salsa and ranch dressing)"
  - "8 burrito-sized whole wheat tortillas"
instructions:
  - "Place chicken and Southwest Chicken Marinade in a gallon freezer bag or container, seal, and marinate in the refrigerator for 2 to 24 hours."
  - "Preheat grill to medium-high heat and grill chicken for 4 to 5 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Let rest on a cutting board, then dice into bite-sized pieces."
  - "Assemble each tortilla with grilled chicken, crumbled bacon, 2 tablespoons shredded cheese, shredded lettuce, diced tomatoes, and 2 tablespoons Creamy Southwest Ranch Sauce. Roll up burrito-style and serve."
