---
title: "Ribs"
description: "Tender Instant Pot baby back ribs finished under the broiler with BBQ sauce."
category: "Dinner"
cookTime: "PT1H30M"
servings: "6 servings"
credit: "https://cincyshopper.com/instant-pot-ribs/"
ingredients:
  - "2 tbsp Kosher Salt"
  - "2 tbsp Brown Sugar"
  - "1 tsp Black Pepper"
  - "1 tsp Garlic Powder"
  - "1 tsp Onion Powder"
  - "1 tsp Chili Powder"
  - "1 tsp Crushed Red Pepper"
  - "1 rack Baby Back Ribs"
  - "12 oz can Coke"
  - "¼ tsp Liquid Smoke"
  - "1 cup BBQ Sauce"
instructions:
  - "Combine the first 7 spice ingredients to make a dry rub and set aside."
  - "Remove the membrane from the back side of the ribs by loosening at one end with a knife and pulling off with a paper towel."
  - "Apply the rub to both sides of the ribs and place in a ring inside the Instant Pot."
  - "Pour the Coke and Liquid Smoke into the center of the pot."
  - "Pressure cook on manual high for 30 minutes with Keep Warm turned off, then allow pressure to release naturally."
  - "Preheat oven to 400 degrees F."
  - "Transfer ribs to a broiler pan and brush generously with BBQ sauce."
  - "Bake for 10 to 15 minutes, watching closely so the sauce caramelizes without burning."
