---
title: "Twice Baked Potatoes"
description: "Loaded twice-baked potatoes stuffed with pulled pork, ranch, sour cream, bacon, and cheese."
category: "Dinner"
cookTime: "PT1H40M"
servings: "6 servings"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "6 big potatoes"
  - "¼ cup ranch dressing"
  - "1 bag shredded cheese"
  - "1 cup sour cream"
  - "½ stick butter"
  - "1 pack bacon bits"
  - "pulled pork"
instructions:
  - "Bake the potatoes at 400 degrees for 1 hour until tender."
  - "Cut each potato in half lengthwise and scoop out the insides, leaving a sturdy shell."
  - "Mix the scooped potato filling together with the ranch, shredded cheese, sour cream, butter, bacon bits, and pulled pork."
  - "Spoon the mixture back into the potato shells and bake at 350 degrees for 30 minutes."
  - "Let sit for 3 minutes before serving."
---
