---
title: "Shepherds Salad"
description: "Crisp Mediterranean chopped tomato, cucumber, pepper, and parsley salad."
category: "Sides"
cookTime: "PT20M"
servings: "8 servings"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "3 tbsp Vera Jane's extra-virgin olive oil"
  - "1 ½ tbsp lemon juice"
  - "¾ tsp sea salt"
  - "freshly ground black pepper, to taste"
  - "2 tomatoes, chopped"
  - "1 cucumber, chopped"
  - "1 cubanelle or banana pepper, chopped"
  - "¼ cup red onion, finely chopped"
  - "½ cup flat-leaf parsley, chopped"
instructions:
  - "To make the dressing, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, sea salt, and black pepper in a bowl."
  - "Combine chopped tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, red onion, and parsley in a serving bowl."
  - "Toss the vegetables with the dressing."
  - "Let stand for 10 minutes before serving so the flavors meld."
---
