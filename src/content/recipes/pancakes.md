---
title: "Pancakes"
description: "Fluffy, classic weekend pancakes with melted butter and vanilla."
category: "Breakfast"
cookTime: "PT10M"
servings: "4 servings"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "1 ½ cup all purpose flour"
  - "3 tsp baking powder"
  - "½ tsp salt"
  - "2 tbsp sugar"
  - "1 cup milk"
  - "1 egg"
  - "1 tsp pure vanilla extract"
  - "3 tbsp butter, melted"
instructions:
  - "In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, salt and sugar."
  - "In a separate bowl, mix together milk, egg and vanilla."
  - "Add milk and egg mixture to the dry ingredients and stir until combined. Don't over mix; some lumps are okay."
  - "Stir in the melted butter, making sure the butter is melted but not hot."
  - "Heat frying pan or griddle to medium heat and lightly oil the pan."
  - "Pour batter into evenly sized pancakes. When golden brown on the bottom, flip and cook the other side until nicely browned, about 2 minutes."
---
