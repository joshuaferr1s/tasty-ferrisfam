---
title: "Sausage Skillet"
description: "One-pan kielbasa, crispy bacon, and garlic-herb broccoli skillet."
category: "Dinner"
cookTime: "PT20M"
servings: "1 serving"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "3 slices thin-sliced bacon, cut into bite-size pieces"
  - "1 bunch broccoli, florets cut into bite-size pieces"
  - "2 polish sausage links, sliced into bite-size pieces"
  - "1 square Land O Lakes Sauté Express (Garlic & Herb)"
instructions:
  - "Fry bacon in a nonstick pan until crisp, then remove the bacon while leaving the grease in the pan."
  - "Add the Sauté Express square to the pan and stir quickly to melt."
  - "Toss in the broccoli florets and stir continuously until nearly tender."
  - "Add the sliced polish sausage and stir for another 1 to 2 minutes until heated through. Serve hot (optionally topped with shredded cheese)."
---
