---
title: "Pulled Pork"
description: "Slow-cooked savory pulled pork simmered in a rich tomato and cooking wine sauce."
category: "Dinner"
cookTime: "PT10H"
servings: "8 servings"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "3-4 lb boneless pork roast"
  - "1 can tomato sauce"
  - "¾ soup can of milk"
  - "1 soup can of burgundy cooking wine"
  - "1 packet dry onion soup mix"
instructions:
  - "In a bowl, mix together the tomato sauce, milk, burgundy cooking wine, and packet of dry onion soup mix."
  - "Pour the mixture over the pork in a slow cooker and cook on low for 8 to 10 hours."
  - "Once done, shred the pork with forks and return it to the sauce briefly to soak up the flavor before serving."
---
