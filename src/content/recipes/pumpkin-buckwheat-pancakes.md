---
title: "Pumpkin Buckwheat Pancakes"
description: "Single-serve spiced pumpkin and buckwheat protein pancakes."
category: "Breakfast"
cookTime: "PT30M"
servings: "1 serving"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "½ cup canned pumpkin puree"
  - "2 large egg whites, whisked"
  - "1 teaspoon maple syrup or honey"
  - "1 teaspoon cinnamon"
  - "⅛ teaspoon baking soda"
  - "2 tablespoons buckwheat flour"
  - "Optional: 1 tablespoon cacao nibs"
instructions:
  - "Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat with a little oil or butter."
  - "Mix all ingredients together in a mixing bowl."
  - "Whisk until the batter is smooth."
  - "Cook pancakes of your preferred size until set and lightly browned on both sides."
