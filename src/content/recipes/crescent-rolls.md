---
title: "Homemade Gluten Free Crescent Rolls"
description: "Soft, buttery homemade gluten-free crescent rolls."
category: "Bread & Baking"
cookTime: "PT1H40M"
servings: "6 servings"
credit: "https://miglutenfreegal.com/gluten-free-crescent-rolls/"
ingredients:
  - "1 pkg active yeast"
  - "6 tbsp warm water"
  - "4 tbsp sugar"
  - "1 ¾ cup gluten free flour"
  - "¼ c. butter, softened, cut into small pieces"
  - "1 egg"
  - "½ tsp salt"
  - "additional 2 tbsp softened butter for spreading and basting"
instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 200 degrees to create a warm proofing environment, then turn it off once it reaches 200 degrees prior to putting the dough in."
  - "Proof your yeast by mixing the yeast and 1 tbsp sugar together in a glass measuring cup, adding warm water, and allowing to proof for 5-10 minutes until doubled in size. (If using instant rise yeast, skip proofing and add dry yeast directly to flour and sugar.)"
  - "In a stand mixer, combine the flour and chunks of softened butter using the paddle attachment until incorporated without large chunks."
  - "Add the egg, salt, 3 tbsp sugar, and proofed yeast (or warm water if using instant yeast). Turn stand mixer on low until all ingredients mix together and a dough forms."
  - "Confirm the oven is turned off."
  - "Place dough on a lightly floured surface. Using a rolling pin, roll into a 12 inch circle. Gently spread approximately 2 tbsp softened butter on top of dough."
  - "Cut dough into 12 (or 8) sections. Working from the outside, roll each individual triangle towards the center to create your crescent. Place on a parchment paper lined cookie sheet, and repeat with remaining dough."
  - "Place rolls in the warmed (turned off) oven, cover with a towel, and allow to rise for 30-45 minutes."
  - "After rolls have risen to almost double their size, remove rolls from oven, and preheat oven to 350 degrees. While oven is heating, brush each roll with a thin coat of butter or oil."
  - "Once oven comes to temperature, bake rolls for 12-15 minutes, rotating pans halfway through."
  - "Remove from oven and serve warm, brushing with any remaining melted butter if desired. Freeze any remaining rolls for later."
---
