---
title: "Tapioca Bread"
description: "Chewy, savory gluten-free Brazilian-style tapioca flatbread."
category: "Bread & Baking"
cookTime: "PT30M"
servings: "1 serving"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "1 cup tapioca flour"
  - "2 tbsp olive oil"
  - "2 tbsp water"
  - "1 egg"
  - "dried basil, to taste"
  - "salt, to taste"
  - "garlic powder, to taste"
  - "onion powder, to taste"
instructions:
  - "Preheat the oven to 400 degrees."
  - "Pour tapioca flour and dried seasonings into a medium mixing bowl."
  - "Add the egg, olive oil, and water."
  - "Mix thoroughly until the batter smooths out into a pourable dough mixture."
  - "Pour onto a baking sheet lined with a nonstick mat or parchment and bake for 15 to 20 minutes until the edges turn golden brown."
---
