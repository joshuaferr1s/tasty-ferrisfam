---
title: "Pork Tenderloin"
description: "Sweet and tangy soy-vinegar marinated pork tenderloin medallions."
category: "Dinner"
cookTime: "PT2H"
servings: "4 servings"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "3 lb boneless pork tenderloin"
  - "1 cup soy sauce"
  - "½ cup white vinegar"
  - "4 minced garlic cloves"
  - "⅓ cup sugar"
  - "a sprinkle or two of ginger"
  - "a sprinkle or two of seasoned salt"
instructions:
  - "Combine all marinade ingredients and microwave briefly to dissolve the sugar."
  - "Cut the pork tenderloin into smaller medallion pieces so they marinate faster."
  - "Marinate the pork for at least 1 hour (longer is better)."
  - "Grill or bake in the oven at 375-400 degrees for 30-40 minutes."
---
