---
title: "Cranberry Sauce"
description: "Simple, naturally sweetened homemade cranberry sauce."
category: "Sides"
cookTime: "PT10M"
servings: "6 servings"
credit: "https://ren.ferrisfam.com"
ingredients:
  - "1 bag cranberries"
  - "½ cup water"
  - "½ cup or less honey"
instructions:
  - "Pick overly soft or shriveled cranberries out of the bag."
  - "Wash cranberries."
  - "Place in pan with water and honey (or other desired sweetener) on a low/medium heat."
  - "Stir regularly until the cranberry sauce reaches desired thickness."
